## Starting assumption

A flying network node is really two systems sharing a power and mass budget. Treating them separately at first should make tradeoffs easier to see.

## Questions

- How much mass can the communications payload consume?
- What antenna placement survives the airframe geometry?
- Which node functions must remain available after landing?
- Does the aircraft need to stay airborne to provide useful coverage?
