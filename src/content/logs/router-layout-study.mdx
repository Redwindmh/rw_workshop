## Packaging before cosmetics

The first enclosure study is about internal relationships rather than appearance. Antenna routing, cable bend radius, access to storage, thermal paths, and the ability to replace the Linux host all matter more than surface finish.

A good field enclosure should make the normal maintenance operations obvious when opened.
