## Result

The access-point side of the node is now behaving predictably enough to stop treating basic Wi-Fi setup as an experimental variable.

## What mattered

The important outcome was not merely getting a client connected once. The configuration needed to survive a restart, remain easy to inspect remotely, and avoid accidentally introducing a default route that would distort later network tests.
