## Why the RFC matters

The project has enough surface area that a vague architecture would let every subsystem absorb responsibilities from every other subsystem.

The revised overview treats the specification as a contract: source knowledge is authored, the compiler validates and transforms it, runtime packages are immutable, and applications consume those packages rather than editing them in place.
