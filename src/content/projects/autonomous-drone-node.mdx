## Research question

Can a small unmanned platform carry useful communications infrastructure without turning the aircraft into a fragile science project?

## Areas to resolve

The radio link is only one part of the problem. Power draw, vibration, antenna placement, mass, flight time, regulatory limits, thermal behavior, recovery, and autonomous positioning all interact.

## Near-term work

This project remains in research while the stationary field-router design matures. The network node should become reliable on the bench before mobility is added as another source of failure.
