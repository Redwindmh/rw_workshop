## Premise

Books, courses, websites, and reference pages are usually treated as the primary container of knowledge. This project reverses that relationship: structured concepts are primary, while documents and learning experiences become compiled views.

## Working rules

- specifications precede implementation;
- concepts have stable identities;
- relationships are explicit;
- generated runtime packages are immutable;
- presentation formats are downstream of the knowledge graph;
- AI may help navigate or transform knowledge, but it does not silently become authoritative source material.

## Current phase

The work is still intentionally foundational: terminology, repository boundaries, RFCs, and the contract between source knowledge and compiled runtime artifacts.
